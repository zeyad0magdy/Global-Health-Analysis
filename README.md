## Description:
This project is a strategic analytical solution, designed to transform a decade of complex global health data into a clear and compelling story. The core of the dashboard is a 12-year time-series analysis (from 2010 to 2022), allowing for the tracking of developments, gaps, and successes in the global health sector.

The comprehensive dashboard is structured into specialized analytical pages to provide an integrated view:
* **Vital Indicator Trends:** This page presents the overall narrative through time-series charts that highlight the significant decrease in infant and maternal mortality rates over the decade and tracks the evolution of health infrastructure by monitoring the year-over-year increase in doctors and hospital beds.
* **Life Expectancy Analysis:** This focuses on the ultimate health outcome, demonstrating the continuous upward trend in life expectancy and revealing the strong inverse correlation between increased vaccination coverage and decreased infant mortality.
* **Mortality & Expenditure Deep Dive:** This provides a granular look at the most affected countries and regions, and uses scatter plots to explore the complex relationship between per-capita health expenditure and the level of mortality reduction.
* **Executive KPI Summary:** This page functions as an executive control panel, rich with Key Performance Indicator (KPI) cards that summarize the global health situation, including Max/Min Life Expectancy (89.9 vs 61.4 years) and Average Vaccination Coverage (88.7%).

---

## 💡 Outcomes & Strategic Impact:
* **Measuring Progress & Identifying Long-Term Trends:** Through the 12-year time-series analysis, the dashboard enables stakeholders to accurately and reliably measure the true impact of past health investments and policies.
* **Enabling Evidence-Based Policymaking:** It provides visual and quantitative proof of public health successes (such as the link between vaccinations and lower mortality), helping to secure funding and guide future policies.
* **Highlighting Critical Disparities for Effective Aid:** By identifying regions and countries with the poorest health outcomes, the dashboard allows organizations to direct humanitarian aid and resources more intelligently and effectively.
* **Linking Investment to Outcomes:** By analyzing the relationship between health expenditure and outcomes (like maternal mortality), the dashboard helps evaluate the effectiveness of health investments and create more efficient national strategies.

## 🔬 Methodology & Next Steps:
Comprehensive data cleaning and transformation (ETL) processes were performed using Power Query to ensure data accuracy and integrity. The current analysis proves strong correlations; the proposed next step is to build a statistical model to test for causation and forecast future indicators.
